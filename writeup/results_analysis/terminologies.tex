\hline
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning in this study} \\
\hline
EM & Exact match between the normalised generated and reference answers. \\
F1 & Harmonic mean of precision and recall. \\
Token F1 & F1 based on token overlap between generated and reference answers. \\
Precision & Proportion of predicted items matched to the reference under the metric's matching rule. \\
Recall & Proportion of reference items matched by the prediction under the metric's matching rule. \\
Hit@$k$ & Whether at least one annotated evidence page occurs within the first $k$ retrieved results. \\
Recall@5 & Proportion of annotated evidence pages covered by the final five retrieved chunks. \\
MRR@5 & Mean reciprocal rank of the first annotated-page hit within the final five results; zero when none is found. \\
Numeric accuracy & Question-level recall of reference numeric entities; a numeric-matching proxy. \\
BERTScore F1 & Contextual token-similarity F1 between generated and reference answers; here, BERT-base layer 9 without baseline rescaling. \\
Macro mean & Unweighted mean of per-question scores over the applicable questions. \\
Conditional mean & Mean over a stated subset, such as valid scored responses without an exact abstention. \\

\hline
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning in this study} \\
\hline
Reference answer / gold answer & Annotated FinanceBench answer used as the comparison reference. \\
Gold evidence & Annotated FinanceBench evidence supporting the reference answer. \\
Generated factual unit & A factual claim extracted from a generated answer for evidence-support assessment. \\
Reference unit & Component of a model-generated reference rubric, intended to represent required answer content. \\
Reference rubric & A fixed set of reference units generated for one question and shared across all four configurations. \\
Whole-answer correctness & Binary model judgment that all required rubric units are answered without a substantive contradiction. Exact abstentions receive zero deterministically. \\
Reference-unit coverage proxy & Fraction of generated reference units judged correctly answered; the reported completeness proxy. \\
Context support & Fraction of scored generated factual units judged supported by the final retrieved chunks. \\
Gold support & Fraction of scored generated factual units judged supported by annotated FinanceBench evidence. \\
Hallucination proxy & One minus the corresponding support score: the fraction of scored units judged unsupported, rather than independently verified false. \\
Partially correct & A substantive answer covering at least one required reference unit, with others missing and no incorrect required unit or substantive contradiction. \\
Valid semantic result & A deterministic exact-abstention score or an accepted model judgment passing the evaluation's consistency checks. \\

\end{tabular}%

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Abstention, scoring coverage and unresolved-judgment terminology.}
\label{tab:abstention-terminology}
{\small
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.08}
\begin{tabular}{|p{0.27\columnwidth}|p{0.66\columnwidth}|}
\hline
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Meaning in this study} \\
\hline
Exact abstention & Entire normalised response matches one of the prescribed refusal phrases. \\
Non-exact-abstention & A response outside the exact-abstention detector; it can still be incomplete, incorrect or a broader refusal. \\
Failure to answer & Outcome assigned to an exact abstention or judged for another response supplying no substantive requested answer. \\
Judge error & Unresolved invalid or inconsistent evaluation output after the retry limit; semantic scores remain missing. \\
Scoring coverage & Number or proportion of responses with a valid score for a particular metric; distinct from answer-content coverage. \\
Correctness bounds & Full-question lower and upper rates obtained by assigning unresolved judgments zero and one, respectively. These are not confidence intervals. \\
Jaccard overlap & Number of shared abstentions divided by the number of questions on which either configuration abstained. \\
Gold-page hit & At least one retrieved chunk comes from an annotated evidence page; this does not establish sufficient answer-bearing passage coverage. \\
\hline
\end{tabular}%
}
\end{table}
